Discord Bot: Enrolment Panel Guide

This knowledge base helps you navigate the Discord Enrolment Panel and select the correct form using the embedded message buttons. Follow the instructions below to ensure your enrolment process is smooth and efficient.

1. What is the Enrolment Panel?

The Enrolment Panel is an interactive embedded message displayed by the Discord bot. It allows students to select the appropriate form for their enrolment status using clearly labeled buttons. Each user sees a personalized view to ensure privacy and clarity.

2. What are the different forms?

  • VIP Enrolment: For VIP students who require priority scheduling and coach assignments.
  • Elite Enrolment: For students with elite status, offering specialized enrolment options.
  • Regular Enrolment: For standard students onboarding into the program.

Each button in the embed corresponds to one of these forms. Make sure you select the one that matches your status.

3. How do I open the form links?

Simply click the button corresponding to your enrolment type. The bot will open the link in your browser, where you can complete the form with your personal information. No additional navigation is required.

4. What if I am unsure which form to select?

If you are uncertain which enrolment form applies to you, please consult the descriptions above carefully. You can also reach out to our staff directly for guidance — they are available to help you choose the correct option.

5. What is the Join / Leave Help Chat button?

This button toggles your access to the #help channel. In that channel, you can receive assistance from our AI bot or communicate directly with staff for support regarding the enrolment process.

6. Can I change my form after selecting a button?

If you accidentally click the wrong button, simply return to the Enrolment Panel and select the correct option. The previous selection does not submit the form until you complete it in your browser.

7. Are my submissions secure?

Yes. All forms are hosted on Airtable and each submission is linked only to your account. The bot itself does not store personal information — it only directs you to the correct form.

8. Who do I contact for further help?

If you encounter any issues with the buttons, form access, or submissions, please message a staff member directly in Discord or use the #help channel. Our team is available to assist you promptly.
