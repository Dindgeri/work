Discord Bot: Partner Connection Panel Guide

This knowledge base explains how to use the Discord Partner Connection Panel. The panel helps you connect with the right service partner by selecting the appropriate option and completing a short form.

1. What is the Partner Connection Panel?

The Partner Connection Panel is an interactive embedded message posted by the Discord bot. It displays multiple buttons, each representing a specific service. Clicking a button opens a short Airtable form in your browser.

Each user interacts privately with the panel, ensuring a clear and secure experience.

2. What happens when I click a service button?

When you select a service, a short form opens where you will:

  • Select the exact service you’re looking for
  • Provide your contact information
  • Enter your Discord username

Once submitted, you will be added to a group chat with the relevant partner within 24 hours.

3. Available service options

Each button in the embed corresponds to one of the following services:

  • Fulfillment – Open form
  • Email Marketing – Open form
  • Customer Service – Open form
  • Bookkeeping – Open form
  • Company Set-up – Open form
  • Ad Accounts – Open form
  • Media Buying – Open form
  • Web Development – Open form
  • Static Ad Agency – Open form
  • Other – Open form

4. What if I’m unsure which option to choose?

If you are not sure which service fits your needs, select the option that most closely matches your situation, or choose Other and describe your request in the form.

You can also request help before submitting by joining the support channel.

5. What does the green Help button do?

The green button allows you to join or leave the support channel. Inside this channel, you can:

  • Get assistance from the AI support bot
  • Ask questions directly to staff members
  • Receive guidance on choosing the correct service

6. Can I submit more than one form?

Yes. If you need help with multiple services, simply return to the panel and submit another form. Each submission is handled independently.

7. Are my submissions secure?

Yes. All forms are securely hosted on Airtable. The Discord bot does not store personal data — it only directs you to the correct form. Each submission is linked solely to the information you provide.

8. Who do I contact if something goes wrong?

If a form does not open, a button does not respond, or you do not hear back within 24 hours, please contact a staff member directly on Discord or use the support channel for assistance.
